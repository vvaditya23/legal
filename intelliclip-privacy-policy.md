## Information we may collect

We collect information about you, including information that directly or indirectly identifies you, if you choose to share it with us. We receive information in a few different ways, including when you contact us or use the Services.

### Contact information

When you contact us, we use your contact information and the content of your message so we can respond to your support requests and comments.

### Clipboard and content information

IntelliClip is a macOS utility that helps you take actions based on content copied to your clipboard. Clipboard content is processed on your Mac to identify useful actions and provide features such as working with URLs, phone numbers, dates and events, addresses, JSON, and GitHub repository URLs.

Your clipboard content is processed locally on your device. IntelliClip's on-device intelligence may use Apple's Foundation Models and Apple Intelligence capabilities when available. We do not otherwise access, collect, or store your clipboard contents on our servers.

IntelliClip may maintain clipboard history on your device so that you can review or delete previous clips. This locally stored clipboard history remains on your Mac unless you choose to share or export content through an action provided by IntelliClip or another application.

When an action opens or hands content to another application, such as Maps, Calendar, Contacts, Reading List, Messages, Phone-related functionality, or Terminal, the content is handled by that application according to its own privacy policy and terms.

### Technical information

We may collect limited technical information from your Mac when you use the Services, such as app version, operating system version, crash reports, log files, and diagnostics, where these are enabled or provided to us. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve IntelliClip.

### Other information

We may collect information from you through third parties, such as when we collect your feedback through surveys or support services.
